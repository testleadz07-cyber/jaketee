# Jacketee care block library

Fourteen original blocks plus three blocks for owner-confirmed blends and self-fabric lining. Use only for confirmed materials. Follow the complete garment label and the most delicate component.

## Melton Wool

Lift loose dust with a soft clothes brush, working gently along the surface. Blot small spills promptly with a clean cloth rather than rubbing. Have persistent marks assessed by a cleaner experienced with wool and any attached trims. Let damp wool dry naturally with its shape supported, then store it clean and dry with room for air to circulate.

- **Do:** Brush lightly after wear to remove surface dust.
- **Do:** Support the shoulders on a broad hanger for storage.
- **Avoid:** Avoid soaking, wringing or machine washing unless the garment label expressly permits it.
- **Avoid:** Avoid bleach, tumble drying and direct heat.

## Cowhide Leather

Wipe smooth cowhide with a soft, dry cloth to remove dust. For a small mark, use a leather cleaner suitable for the finish only after testing an inconspicuous area. If the leather becomes damp, blot gently and allow it to dry at room temperature. Store on a broad hanger in a ventilated place, away from sunlight.

- **Do:** Check that any cleaner is intended for finished cowhide.
- **Do:** Let the leather dry completely before putting the jacket away.
- **Avoid:** Avoid machine washing, soaking and bleach.
- **Avoid:** Avoid tumble drying, radiators and direct ironing.

## Sheep / Lambskin Leather

Remove dust from smooth sheep or lambskin leather with a very soft, dry cloth. Blot a spill with light pressure and ask a leather-care specialist about any remaining stain. Let damp areas dry naturally at room temperature without stretching the leather. Keep the garment on a broad hanger with space around it so the delicate surface is not pressed against other clothing.

- **Do:** Use light pressure when handling or wiping the surface.
- **Do:** Check products with a specialist and test a hidden area before use.
- **Avoid:** Avoid soaking, machine washing and bleach.
- **Avoid:** Avoid tumble drying, direct heat and abrasive brushes.

## Suede

Let goat or cow suede dry before lifting loose dirt with a suede brush. Use gentle strokes and stop if the surface begins to change. Take greasy marks, water rings or stubborn stains to a suede-cleaning specialist. Air dry away from heat and store in a dry, ventilated space where the nap will not be crushed.

- **Do:** Choose tools specifically made for suede.
- **Do:** Keep the garment away from rain where possible.
- **Avoid:** Avoid smooth-leather creams, oils and polish.
- **Avoid:** Avoid soaking, bleach, tumble drying and direct heat.

## Faux / PU Leather

Remove surface dirt with a soft cloth barely dampened with water. If the label permits, use a little mild soap on the cloth and wipe away the residue. Let the coating air dry at room temperature before storing. Hang loosely in a cool, dry place to reduce creasing and surface sticking.

- **Do:** Test cleaning on an inconspicuous area first.
- **Do:** Dry the surface fully before covering or storing it.
- **Avoid:** Avoid leather oils, solvents and harsh stain removers.
- **Avoid:** Avoid bleach, soaking, tumble drying and direct heat.

## Polyester Satin

Blot marks gently so the smooth satin surface is not scuffed. If the complete garment label allows washing, use cool water and a mild detergent on a gentle setting. Let the garment air dry with its shape supported and away from direct heat. Store on a smooth hanger where rough fasteners cannot catch the fabric.

- **Do:** Close fasteners and turn the garment inside out before permitted washing.
- **Do:** Wash with similar colors and smooth fabrics.
- **Avoid:** Avoid bleach and vigorous rubbing.
- **Avoid:** Avoid wringing and hot ironing on the satin surface.

## Nylon

Wipe away light dirt with a soft, damp cloth. When the complete garment label permits washing, use cool water, mild detergent and gentle movement. Rinse as directed on the label and air dry away from heat. Store clean and fully dry without compressing any coated areas for long periods.

- **Do:** Close zips and other fasteners before permitted washing.
- **Do:** Check for coating-specific instructions on the garment label.
- **Avoid:** Avoid bleach and fabric softener on coated nylon.
- **Avoid:** Avoid hot ironing and drying near heaters.

## Soft-Shell Fabric

Brush off loose dirt and wipe small marks with a soft, damp cloth. If the garment label permits washing, choose a gentle cycle and a cleaner suitable for its technical finish. Air dry with zips open and pockets empty unless the label gives a different method. Store dry and loosely hung so the fabric and any bonded layers are not held in sharp folds.

- **Do:** Follow instructions for the specific membrane or coating, if present.
- **Do:** Remove detergent residue with the rinse method stated on the label.
- **Avoid:** Avoid bleach, fabric softener and unapproved solvents.
- **Avoid:** Avoid direct heat or ironing unless the garment label expressly allows it.

## Cotton Fleece

Treat small marks gently with a little mild detergent if the garment label allows it. Wash in cool water with similar colors when the complete garment is washable. Reshape while damp and air dry with the weight of the fabric supported. Store fully dry, folded without heavy items pressing on the fleece.

- **Do:** Turn the garment inside out before permitted washing.
- **Do:** Use gentle movement to help protect the fleece surface.
- **Avoid:** Avoid bleach and aggressive stain scrubbing.
- **Avoid:** Avoid hot washing and direct heat that can shrink the fabric.

## Cotton Twill

Blot fresh marks rather than grinding them into the weave. If washing is permitted for the complete garment, use cool water and mild detergent with similar colors. Smooth the fabric while damp and let it air dry with its shape supported. Store dry on a hanger or folded along broad, gentle folds.

- **Do:** Check whether dark colors need a separate first wash.
- **Do:** Fasten zips and turn the garment inside out before permitted washing.
- **Avoid:** Avoid bleach and strong stain removers on colored fabric.
- **Avoid:** Avoid hot washing, wringing and prolonged direct sunlight.

## Denim

Lift surface dirt and spot clean gently between washes. When the complete garment label permits washing, turn the jacket inside out and use cool water with mild detergent. Reshape and air dry in shade to limit uneven fading. Store fully dry, and keep dark denim from rubbing against pale fabrics while damp.

- **Do:** Wash dark denim separately or with similar dark colors.
- **Do:** Smooth seams and pockets before air drying.
- **Avoid:** Avoid bleach and abrasive stain scrubbing.
- **Avoid:** Avoid hot washing and leaving wet denim folded in a pile.

## Puffer / Down or Synthetic Fill

Clean insulated garments only by the method allowed for the complete shell, fill and trims. If washing is permitted, use a cleaner appropriate to the confirmed fill and handle the padding gently. Air dry only when the label permits that method, allowing the filling to dry completely throughout before wear or storage. If the label requires specialist drying, use a qualified cleaner rather than improvising at home. Store dry and loosely hung instead of tightly compressed.

- **Do:** Confirm whether the fill is down or synthetic before selecting a cleaner.
- **Do:** Check seams and padded areas for remaining dampness before storage.
- **Avoid:** Avoid bleach, high heat and tumble drying.
- **Avoid:** Avoid wringing the padding or storing it while damp.

## Quilted / Polyester Lining

Air the inside of the jacket after wear and blot small lining marks with a soft cloth. Use minimal moisture only where the garment label permits it, keeping the outer fabric and trims protected. Do not wash an attached lining separately from the rest of the garment. Let the lining and any padding dry fully at room temperature, then store with air space around the jacket.

- **Do:** Follow the care method for the complete jacket, including its most delicate material.
- **Do:** Open the jacket to help the lining air after wear.
- **Avoid:** Avoid soaking the lining or pulling at quilted stitches.
- **Avoid:** Avoid bleach and direct heat on the lining or padding.

## Chenille Patches and Embroidery

Lift dust from lettering and stitched designs gently with a soft, dry cloth. Blot small marks without rubbing loops, threads or patch edges. If the decorated garment is washable, follow its label and turn it inside out to reduce snagging. Let decorations air dry naturally and store without heavy folds pressing across raised lettering.

- **Do:** Check patch edges and loose threads before cleaning.
- **Do:** Keep zips, hooks and rough fabrics away from raised stitching.
- **Avoid:** Avoid bleach, stiff brushes and pulling loose threads.
- **Avoid:** Avoid direct ironing, high heat and soaking glued patch areas.

## Melton Wool / Polyester Blend

Remove loose dust with a soft clothes brush and blot small spills without rubbing. Follow the complete garment label before using water or a cleaning product, especially where leather or decorations are attached. Let damp fabric dry naturally with its shape supported. Store clean and fully dry on a broad hanger with space around the jacket.

- **Do:** Use gentle brushing to protect the surface.
- **Do:** Tell a specialist cleaner about every attached material.
- **Avoid:** Avoid bleach, soaking and vigorous rubbing.
- **Avoid:** Avoid tumble drying and direct heat.

## Cotton / Polyester Fleece

Blot marks gently rather than scrubbing the brushed surface. If the complete garment label permits washing, use cool water and mild detergent with similar colors. Reshape while damp and air dry with the weight supported. Fold only when fully dry and keep heavy items away from raised decorations.

- **Do:** Turn the garment inside out before permitted washing.
- **Do:** Close fasteners to reduce snagging.
- **Avoid:** Avoid bleach and harsh stain removers.
- **Avoid:** Avoid hot washing and direct heat.

## Lining in the Same Fabric as the Body

Air the inside of the jacket after wear and remove loose dust gently. Follow the body fabric care instructions together with any stricter requirements for sleeves, fill or decoration. Do not wash the attached lining separately or soak moisture through to other materials. Let the entire garment dry fully before storing it loosely in a ventilated place.

- **Do:** Open the jacket to help the lining air.
- **Do:** Use the care method for the most delicate attached component.
- **Avoid:** Avoid bleach and aggressive rubbing inside the jacket.
- **Avoid:** Avoid direct heat and storage while damp.
