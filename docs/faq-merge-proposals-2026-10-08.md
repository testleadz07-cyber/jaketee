## 3. Cancel or change an order

### Before

**Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?** (fallback)

**Can Jacketee cancel my order?** (database, 6a9efd2a0faef0e1af3011c4)

Yes, Jacketee reserves the right to cancel an order under specific circumstances. If the uploaded artwork is deemed not feasible for production, if attempts to communicate with the customer regarding the order go unanswered, or if there's a discrepancy between the billing and shipping addresses that aren't rectified through verification, Jacketee may cancel the order.

Additionally, in instances where the artwork is excessively complex, contains too many colors or gradients that are unattainable for embroidery but could be achieved through sublimation printing, Jacketee holds the right to cancel the order if the customer insists on embroidery for such designs. These conditions are in place to ensure the quality and feasibility of the final product.

**How can I cancel my order?** (database, 6a9efd2c0faef0e1af3011d3)

To cancel an order, please contact us within 24 hours via email or Facebook Messenger with your 5-digit order number. There is no cancellation fee within this window. Requests made after 24 hours cannot be accepted, since production may have already begun.

A refund will be issued in a few days.

**Can I request changes after placing my order?** (database, 6a9efd2f0faef0e1af3011e9)

Yes, you can request changes within 24 hours of placing an order. To meet the promised delivery date we process all orders after 24 hours. If you have any change in your order, kindly request changes free of cost within 24 hours by email. Write email subject 5 DIGIT ORDER# CHANGES.

Note: Additional charges may apply if you change materials or add new embroideries / patches.
